# Product

## What this is

**AI Agents: a simple guide**, a talk for an HR team. It explains in plain words what an AI agent is, shows the presenter’s own eight-agent team and Dojima, an app built with an agent, and then proposes an agent team for HR.

## Audience and setting

- HR colleagues who aren't technical. They watch it live on a projector or a shared screen in a meeting room.
- It's presented live with narration, so slides carry few words. The speaker notes hold the script.
- They should leave knowing the difference between a chatbot and an agent, believing it already works (real screenshots), and with one small first step: *one agent, one task, one week*.

## Brief (pinned by the user)

- Minimalist and aesthetic.
- **Black, white and gray only.** No accent color anywhere in the deck's own design. The screenshots keep their original colors because they are evidence.
- Every slide has its own entrance animation.
- All of the draft's material stays: every line of copy, every screenshot, every speaker note. Slides may be restructured, added or merged.

## Deliverables

- `index.html`: the web deck, which is the primary version.
- `pptx/AI-Agents.pptx`: a matching PowerPoint version with native transitions.

## Surface mode

Experience: the slides are the work, so the interface steps back.
